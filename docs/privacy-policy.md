# Pawtillery Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 27, 2026*

Pawtillery is a family game. It is made to be played by children and grown-ups, and it is built to collect as little as possible.

## What the game collects

**Nothing about you.** Pawtillery has no accounts, no sign-in, no chat, no social features and no way to share anything you type, say or photograph. It does not ask for your name, age, email, location, contacts or photos.

Your progress (treats, unlocked pets, stars, settings) is saved **only on your device**. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

## Purchases

The optional one-time purchase, Pawtillery Plus, is handled entirely by **Google Play**. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

A grown-up check (a math question) appears before any purchase and before any link that leaves the game.

## Ads

Pawtillery shows **no banner or pop-up ads**. A player may *choose* to watch a short video ad to double the treats from a game, at most a few times a day. These ads are served by **Google AdMob** and are requested as:

- **child-directed** (treated as directed to children, under COPPA and the GDPR),
- **non-personalized** (no ad targeting based on behavior or interests), and
- **rated G** (general audiences) at most.

To deliver and count an ad, AdMob processes limited technical information such as the device's IP address and type. Google's policy: <https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads>.

Players who buy Pawtillery Plus never see ads.

## Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.
